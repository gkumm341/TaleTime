MASTER IMAGE SPECIFICATION
1. FORMAT & LAYOUT

Aspect ratio: Portrait (2:3)

Recommended resolution:

1024 × 1536 px (standard)

2048 × 3072 px (print-quality upscale)

Page structure:

Either:

Single full-page illustration

OR multi-panel story page (2–4 panels)

Panel rules (if used):

Soft cream paper margins between panels

No hard borders, no frames, no outlines

Panels must feel painted onto the same page

2. ABSOLUTE TEXT RULE (CRITICAL)

NO TEXT WHATSOEVER

No captions

No labels

No banners

No map names

No letters, signs, books, clocks, or objects containing readable characters

No decorative typography

No hidden or implied words

If an object traditionally has text (maps, bottles, signs), it must be blank, symbolic, or purely illustrative.

3. ART STYLE

Medium simulation:

Hand-painted watercolor

Fine pencil or ink linework

Finish:

Soft paper grain visible

Slight pigment bleed

Vintage children’s book illustration aesthetic

Era inspiration:

Late 19th / early 20th century European storybooks

Arthur Rackham / Beatrix Potter–adjacent (but original)

4. COLOR PALETTE (VERY IMPORTANT)

Overall tone: Soft, warm, nostalgic

Primary colors:

Muted sage greens

Olive and moss foliage

Warm straw yellows

Soft sky blues

Creamy off-whites

Secondary colors:

Pale peach

Dusty rose

Light ochre

Restrictions:

No neon

No pure black

No harsh contrast

Shadows must be watercolor-soft, never solid

5. LIGHTING

Natural, diffused light

Golden hour or soft daylight

Gentle gradients

No dramatic lighting

No hard shadows

Calm, peaceful atmosphere

6. CHARACTER DESIGN (ALICE)

Appearance:

Child, approximately 7–9 years old

Soft blonde or light brown hair

Loose curls or gentle waves

Clothing:

Blue dress

White apron

Simple black or brown shoes

Expression:

Curious, gentle, thoughtful

Never exaggerated

Pose:

Natural, story-driven

No modern gestures

7. ENVIRONMENT

Natural landscapes:

Meadows

Rivers

Gardens

Forest paths

Architecture:

Cottage-scale

Stone or wood textures

Rounded, storybook proportions

Backgrounds must feel:

Quiet

Dreamlike

Slightly whimsical but grounded

8. MAP-SPECIFIC RULES (WHEN CREATING MAPS)

Hand-drawn illustrated map

Parchment-style background

Organic paths, rivers, and landmarks

Decorative elements only (trees, flowers, characters)

No compass letters

No location names

No banners or scrolls

Orientation implied visually, not labeled

9. COMPOSITION PRINCIPLES

Balanced but slightly asymmetrical

Eye guided naturally through the scene

No empty negative space that looks modern

Everything should feel hand-placed

10. CONTENT SAFETY & CONSISTENCY

No modern objects

No modern clothing

No typography artifacts

No digital effects

No sharp edges or vector-like shapes
