(A calm bedtime retelling)


On a quiet island called Prince Edward Island, where the fields roll gently and the sea hums in the distance, there stood a small farmhouse called Green Gables.

It belonged to two siblings.

Matthew Cuthbert, kind and shy. And Marilla Cuthbert, serious and strong.

They lived simple lives, and one day they decided they needed a boy to help with the farm.

So Matthew went to the train station.

But when the train arrived…

A girl stepped off instead.

Her name was Anne Shirley.

She had red hair that caught the sunlight. Eyes that sparkled with wonder. And a heart overflowing with words.

{{1.png}}

Anne talked the whole way to Green Gables.

She told Matthew about the places she had lived. The dreams she carried. The hope she held inside her that one day she would belong somewhere.

Matthew listened quietly.

And by the time they reached the house, he already knew…

He could not send her away.

Marilla was surprised when she saw Anne.

She had expected a boy.

But when she looked at Anne’s thin shoulders and hopeful smile, something softened inside her.

And so Anne stayed.

At Green Gables.

At first, everything felt like magic to Anne.

She loved the tall trees along the road and called it the White Way of Delight. She named the pond the Lake of Shining Waters. She whispered goodnight to flowers and stars.

For the first time in her life, Anne felt safe.

Soon, Anne met Diana Barry, a girl with kind eyes and a warm laugh.

From the very first afternoon together, they became bosom friends.

They shared secrets beneath the trees. They promised to be friends forever. They dreamed of wonderful futures.

Anne had never had a best friend before.

And now she had one.

{{2.png}}

But Anne’s imagination often led her into trouble.

Once, she was wrongly accused of taking Marilla’s brooch.

Anne cried alone in her room, learning how painful it felt to be misunderstood.

Another time, in school, a boy named Gilbert Blythe teased her about her red hair.

Anne, full of pride and fire, smashed her slate over his head.

From that day on, she refused to speak to him.

But Gilbert never stopped admiring her courage.

Anne worked hard at school.

She studied late into the night. She dreamed of becoming something more than the lonely child she once was.

With Diana by her side and Gilbert as her quiet rival, Anne grew stronger each year.

As time passed, Anne changed.

She grew taller. Her voice grew calmer. Her heart grew wiser.

But her love for Green Gables never faded.

{{3.png}}

Then one day, sadness came.

Matthew fell ill.

And before anyone was ready…

Matthew passed away.

The house felt different after that.

The rooms were quieter. The evenings were longer. Marilla’s eyes grew tired.

And Anne’s heart grew steady.

Anne had been offered the chance to leave.

To go far away. To continue her studies. To chase her dreams.

But when she looked at Marilla, standing alone in the doorway…

Anne made her choice.

She would stay.

She would care for Marilla. She would teach nearby. She would give back the love she had been given.

And in doing so, Anne learned something very important.

That sometimes, the greatest dream…

Is staying where you are needed most.

{{4.png}}

And so Anne Shirley…

The little girl who once arrived by mistake…

Became the heart of Green Gables.

She grew into a young woman who knew that love was not only found in faraway dreams…

But in quiet kitchens. In familiar roads. In staying when it matters most.

And if you ever walk down a peaceful country lane…

And hear the wind whisper through the trees…

You might just hear Anne’s voice too…

Still dreaming. Still hoping. Still home.
