Treasure Island.


Long ago, by a peaceful seaside road, there stood a small inn called the Admiral Benbow.

It was there that a boy named Jim Hawkins lived with his parents, helping with small chores and listening to the sound of the waves at night.

Jim’s life was simple… until the day a mysterious old sailor arrived.

The sailor was called Billy Bones.

He carried a heavy sea-chest. He sang dark sea songs in a rough voice. And every day, he watched the road as if waiting for danger.

Before long, danger came to him.

Strange men appeared at the inn. One called Black Dog. Another, a terrifying blind man named Pew.

Their visit filled the old sailor with fear.

And soon after, Billy Bones fell ill and died… leaving behind a secret that would change Jim’s life forever.

After the sailor’s death, Jim and his mother opened the sea-chest.

Inside, they found coins… and a bundle of papers wrapped in oilskin.

Jim took the papers to two trusted men in the village.

Dr. Livesey, kind and wise. And Squire Trelawney, brave and generous.

Together, they opened the bundle.

Inside was a map.

A map to buried treasure.

The treasure of the feared pirate Captain Flint.

From that moment, their quiet lives were over.

An adventure had begun.

Soon, a ship was prepared.

It was called the Hispaniola.

The squire hired a crew and a cook who seemed friendly and cheerful.

His name was Long John Silver.

He had only one leg… and a bright smile… and a voice that made people trust him.

Jim liked him at once.

But not everyone is what they seem.

The ship sailed across the wide, quiet sea.

At first, the days were peaceful.

But one afternoon, while hiding in an apple barrel, Jim heard something he was never meant to hear.

Long John Silver was not just a cook.

He was a pirate.

And many of the crew were pirates too.

They planned to steal the treasure… and take the ship.

Jim’s heart pounded, but he stayed brave.

He told Dr. Livesey and the squire everything he had heard.

And quietly… they prepared to protect themselves.

At last, the ship reached a distant island.

A green island with tall trees and hidden beaches.

Treasure Island.

Soon after landing, danger showed itself.

Some of the pirates tried to take control.

There was fear… and confusion… and the sound of raised voices.

The loyal crew escaped to an old wooden fort on the island, called the stockade.

And there they waited… hoping to stay safe.

One day, Jim wandered alone in the woods.

There, he met a strange man dressed in animal skins.

His name was Ben Gunn.

He had been left behind on the island years before.

And he had changed in the quiet loneliness.

Ben Gunn told Jim something amazing.

He had already found Captain Flint’s treasure… and moved it to a safe place.

Hope returned to Jim’s heart.

Meanwhile, Long John Silver was playing a dangerous game.

Sometimes he helped the pirates. Sometimes he helped Jim’s friends.

No one ever knew exactly where his loyalty lay.

But Jim saw something else in him.

Not just cunning… but also a strange, careful kindness.

One night, Jim slipped away.

He found a small boat and rowed through the dark water.

He reached the Hispaniola and fought bravely to take it back from the pirates.

It was dangerous.

But Jim did not give up.

And at last, he returned safely to his friends.

Soon, the time came to search for the treasure.

Pirates and honest men walked together across the island, following the old map.

But when they reached the spot…

The treasure was gone.

The pirates cried out in anger.

But before fear could grow… Dr. Livesey and Ben Gunn appeared.

They led everyone to a hidden cave.

And there…

The treasure lay safely stored.

Long John Silver, seeing his chance, escaped quietly.

He vanished into the world beyond the sea… with a small bag of gold.

And no one ever knew where he went.

The treasure was brought home.

The voyage ended.

Jim returned to the Admiral Benbow…
older…
braver…
and wiser.

But sometimes, at night, when the wind blows just right…

He still hears the echo of a song.

“Fifteen men on the dead man’s chest…”

And remembers the island… the friends… and the adventure that once found him.
